Dear Trishi (chudail Yeon),

Happy birthdayyyyy, pagal! 😭💗 Haan haan, aaj tera special din hai, toh birthday queen ban ja. Bas attitude ka volume thoda control mein rakhna, warna mujhe website ke saath complaint form bhi banana padega.

Sabse pehle, YES, maine tere liye poori website banayi hai. Log ek happy birthday text bhej dete hain, aur idhar main GIF, gifts, cake, photos, letter sab leke baithi hoon. Itna toh bol de: wah Ash, kamaal kar diya. 😌

Tu soch rahi hogi itni mehnat kyun? Kyunki tu ordinary wish wali insaan hi nahi hai. Tu toh ek walking plot twist hai. Ek second mein angel, agle second mein chudail. Aur phir innocent face aise banati hai jaise kuch hua hi nahi.

Waise teri bachpan wali pictures dekh ke laga kitni sweet bacchi thi. Phir present-day Trishi yaad aa gayi. Kya se kya ho gayi, Yeon. Character development kaafi dangerous hai. 😂

But jokes apart, I love that tu apni weirdness hide nahi karti. Kabhi khud pe hasna, kabhi random baat pe serious ho jaana, kabhi full drama queen — somehow sab milke ek hi iconic person banate hain.

Mujhe woh random bakbak wali friendship pasand hai jahan conversation ki koi proper direction hi nahi hoti. Ek baat se dusri baat, phir third topic, aur phir hum khud sochte hain ki ye discussion shuru kahan se hua tha.

Teri ek cheez genuinely achhi lagti hai: jab tu khush hoti hai na, woh excitement contagious lagti hai. Aur jab tu kisi cheez ko lekar excited hoti hai toh poora environment thoda brighter feel hota hai. Ab zyada proud mat ho jaana.

Tu kabhi kabhi mera patience test karti hai, ye bhi sach hai. Tera 'bas do minute' wala time zone NASA ko study karna chahiye. Lekin koi nahi, tere birthday pe saare pending cases temporarily close. Kal se phir courtroom khulega.

Life kabhi easy, kabhi confusing, kabhi straight-up exhausting hoti hai. Main bas chahti hoon ki iss saal tere paas achhe log, achhe moments, aur thoda extra peace ho. Har problem ka solution ek saath milna zaroori nahi.

Aur sun, khud ko kisi aur ke comparison mein mat measure karna. Tera timeline tera hai. Tu apne pace se grow kar, naye things try kar, mistakes se seekh. Aur beech mein apne liye thoda sa time bhi nikaal.

Birthday ki wishes mein sab same line bolte hain: stay blessed, stay happy. Main bhi wish karti hoon, but thoda clearly: mujhe hope hai tu genuinely smile kare, bina kisi pressure ke, aur jo cheezein tere liye matter karti hain woh dheere dheere better hoti jaayein.

Jo dreams tu quietly rakhti hai na, I hope unke liye tujhe confidence mile. Jab tu kisi cheez mein khud pe doubt kare toh yaad rakhna ek tough day tera whole story decide nahi karta.

Kisi bhi din mood off ho toh full entertaining mode mein rehna zaroori nahi. Tujhe boring days, sleepy days, quiet days sab allowed hain. Tu koi 24/7 content creator nahi hai, madam.

Waise meri ek aur complaint hai: tu mujhe itna roast material khud deti hai ki mujhe creative writing ki practice hi nahi karni padti. Main joke sochne se pehle tu kuch funny kar chuki hoti hai. Free entertainment, thank you.

Aaj tera birthday hai toh cake ka sabse bada piece le le. But ek piece mere liye bhi rakhna. Sirf photo bhej ke 'dekhhh' bola toh agle saal surprise website ke badle 80-slide roast presentation milega.

Aur haan, Shinchan ko bhi respect dena. Bechara iss project mein kitni baar dance kar chuka hai. Usko salary nahi mili, sirf mere deadlines mile. Red shirt wala hardworking employee deserves cake.

Photos dekh ke ek baat samajh aati hai: time kitni jaldi nikal jaata hai. Baby pics, school pics, ab wali pics — sab different chapters hain. I hope aage wale chapters mein bohot saari achhi stories likhi jaayein.

Chahe future mein things kitni bhi change ho, main hope karti hoon tu apni khud ki spark kabhi lose na kare. Growth ka matlab boring hona nahi hota. Tu responsible ho sakti hai aur weird bhi. Balance, madam.

Tere aas paas aise log hon jo tujhe genuinely respect karein, jo tere achievements pe khush hon aur jo tujhe sunte bhi hon. Aur jo log sirf unnecessary negativity laayein unke liye exit sign clearly visible ho.

Funny thing ye hai ki main normally itni emotional lines likhne ke baad koi silly joke maar deti hoon. Toh haan, main ab bhi wahi karne wali hoon. Lekin beech ka emotional part sach tha, okay?

Tu mere liye important hai, and I am genuinely glad we're friends. Hamesha perfect words nahi milte, but agar kabhi baat karni ho toh you can tell me. Aur agar bas memes bhejne hain, woh bhi acceptable communication hai.

I hope is saal tu kuch aise moments collect kare jo big events na hoke bhi special lagein — favourite song, random laughter, chhoti achievements, nice conversations, aur woh din jab sab kuch surprisingly peaceful lagta hai.

Ab tak tu shayad scroll karte karte soch rahi hogi: Ash, khatam kab hoga? Answer: pata nahi. Itna likh diya toh thoda aur likh leti hoon. Aur waise bhi tu birthday girl hai, free mein meri bakbak sunne ka golden chance hai.

Seriously though, make a wish, eat something yummy, laugh loudly, take photos, and enjoy your day the way YOU want. Kisi aur ke expectations ke liye perfect birthday plan banane ki koi zaroorat nahi.

Happy birthday once again, chudail Yeon. Hamesha apni cute, chaotic, dramatic aur wonderful self rehna. Aur website pasand aaye toh minimum five-star review, samjhi? 😭💗

Love you, bestie. — Ash ♡
