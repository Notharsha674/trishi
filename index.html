Dear Trishi (chudail Yeon),

Happy birthday, Trishi! Haan, chudail Yeon bhi chalega. Socha simple message bhej dun, but phir idea aaya ki website bana dete hain. Ab bana hi di hai, toh thoda appreciation toh banta hai.

First of all, I hope tera birthday achha jaaye. Good food, achhe log, favourite music, aur aaram se din enjoy kar. Kuch extraordinary karna zaroori nahi. Jo tujhe accha lage, wahi kar.

Purani photos select karte waqt ek baat notice hui: bachpan mein kitni seedhi lag rahi thi. Ab personality mein updates kaafi aa gaye hain. Yeon version 2.0 has entered the chat.

Har photo alag phase ki hai. Kahi school uniform, kahi serious expression, kahi proper attitude. Kuch photos dekhke hasi aayi, aur kuch dekhke bas laga ki time kitni jaldi nikal gaya.

Waise ek ordinary birthday wish ke liye HTML, CSS aur JavaScript use karna thoda extra hai. Mujhe pata hai. But ye fun project bhi tha aur tere liye kuch different karna tha. Ab bugs pakad kar meri class mat lena.

Apni dosti ki funny baat ye hai ki kabhi kisi normal topic pe shuru hoti hai aur do minute mein pata nahi kahan pahunch jaati hai. Sometimes random jokes, sometimes actual discussions, sometimes complete nonsense.

Best part? Har baar perfect conversation karne ki zaroorat nahi. Memes bhej do, kuch ajeeb sa observation share kar do, ya bas thodi bakbak. Dosti mein ye sab normal hona chahiye.

Haan tu kabhi kabhi annoying ho sakti hai. Official review hai ye. But tu funny bhi hai, direct bhi, aur achhi friend bhi. Bas birthday ke naam pe ego ko zyada mat badha lena.

Seriously though, I hope iss year jo bhi plans hain tere, unmein progress mile. Ho sakta hai sab kuch exactly plan ke according na chale, but I hope tu achhi opportunities pakde aur apna pace maintain kare.

Achhe log milen, interesting experiences hon, aur aise moments bhi aayen jahan bina kisi pressure ke just enjoy kar sake. Boring days bhi life ka part hain, unmein kuch galat nahi.

Kabhi things hectic ho jaayein toh break lena. Humesha sab kuch sorted rakhna possible nahi hota. Jo important hai uske liye time nikaal, aur baaki sab mein thoda chill.

Ab roast section. Ye letter itna lamba ho gaya hai ki mujhe khud samajh nahi aa raha ye birthday wish hai ya college assignment. Agar end tak pahunch gayi toh congratulations, patience level unlocked.

Shinchan ne bhi kaafi mehnat ki hai. Banda har screen pe reactions de raha hai. Usko imaginary cake ka ek slice milna chahiye. Website ke team members ki respect karo.

Three gifts rakhe hain: birthday card, photos, aur ye letter. Koi dramatic confession ya filmy twist nahi. Bas ek bestie ka thoda over-engineered birthday surprise.

Toh happy birthday again. I hope this year better ho, and you get more reasons to laugh. Apne plans enjoy kar, achha cake kha, aur gallery ke photos pe complain mat karna.

Aur haan, is website ko rate karna. Agar sirf 'nice' bolke chali gayi toh main samajh jaungi ki tujhe coding effort ka appreciation course karna padega. — Ash

bass bakwas lika hai kuch bhi lik diya.
